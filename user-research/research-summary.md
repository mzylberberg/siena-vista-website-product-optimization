# User Research Summary - Retrospective Project Documentation

## Research Method

I conducted user research through a Google Forms questionnaire designed to 
understand how users perceived and navigated the Siena VISTA website.

The questionnaire focused on:

- Website navigation
- Information discoverability
- User expectations
- Website visibility
- Recruitment-related information

## Research Limitation

The original questionnaire and raw responses are no longer
available. This document reconstructs the research methodology
and major findings based on the work conducted during the project.

## Key Finding

A majority of the feedback indicated that the Siena VISTA website
had limited visibility throughout the VISTA recruitment process.
