### Unclear Role of Siena in the VISTA Experience

Users could be unclear about the role Siena University played in their VISTA experience because the recruitment journey involved multiple organizations.

A typical VISTA journey could involve interactions with:

**AmeriCorps → Siena VISTA → Nonprofit Host Site**

Prospective members often focused on finding and applying for a VISTA position and then interacting with the nonprofit host organization during the recruitment process. As a result, some users did not immediately recognize that accepting a Siena VISTA position also involved a commitment to the Siena VISTA program.

The VISTA experience therefore included two connected components:

**Host Site Responsibilities**
+
**Siena VISTA Program Engagement**

Siena-related activities, including team building and other program activities, were an additional part of the member experience.

### Product Implication

The website needed to clearly communicate:

- Siena's role in the VISTA experience
- The relationship between AmeriCorps, Siena, and the host organization
- What members should expect from the Siena VISTA program
- Why members should continue engaging with the Siena VISTA website after accepting a position
