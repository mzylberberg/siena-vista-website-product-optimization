# Product Opportunity

## From Website to Product

The research shifted the project from a traditional website-maintenance mindset toward a product optimization approach.

The opportunity was not simply to make pages look better or improve individual metrics. The opportunity was to increase the website's usefulness, discoverability, and relevance within the user's journey.

## Opportunity Areas

### 1. Create an Earlier Recruitment Touchpoint

The website could become a place where prospective VISTAs discover available Siena VISTA opportunities during the recruitment process.

### 2. Increase Website Visibility

Create stronger reasons for prospective and incoming VISTAs to visit and return to the Siena VISTA website.

### 3. Clarify Siena's Role

Clearly communicate the relationship between:

**AmeriCorps + Siena University + Nonprofit Host Site**

Users needed to understand that their VISTA experience involved both responsibilities at their host organization and engagement with the Siena VISTA program.

### 4. Improve Information Discoverability

Make important recruitment and program information easier to locate and understand.

### 5. Reduce User Friction

Address content, readability, accessibility, and search visibility issues that could make information harder to find or use.

### 6. Establish Continuous Improvement

Create a repeatable process for identifying, prioritizing, resolving, and measuring website issues.

## Key Product Decision: Open Positions

One of the primary product changes was the creation of an **Open Positions** page.

The page was designed to make Siena VISTA opportunities directly discoverable through the Siena website and give prospective VISTAs a concrete reason to visit the site during the recruitment process.

This addressed the visibility problem by introducing an earlier website touchpoint rather than relying on users to discover the Siena website after already engaging with AmeriCorps or a host organization.

### Intended User Flow

**Discover → Explore → Understand → Apply**

The Open Positions page also created an opportunity to connect individual position listings with clearer information about the Siena VISTA program and the broader member experience.

## Product Strategy

The project followed this model:

**Research → Diagnose → Prioritize → Implement → Measure**

This allowed website improvements to be driven by identified user needs while maintaining measurable operational and governance outcomes.

## Success Measures

Success was evaluated using a combination of:

- SEO improvement
- Issue completion
- Accessibility improvements
- Readability improvements
- Content accuracy
- Information discoverability
- Creation of a new recruitment touchpoint through the Open Positions page
