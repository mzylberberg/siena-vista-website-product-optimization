# Content Implementation

## Objective

Improve the accuracy, clarity, organization, and usefulness of website content 
utilizing the internationally recoginzed Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1

## Approach

Content was reviewed as part of the broader website optimization process.
Pages were evaluated for: 
- Outdated information, 
- Unclear wording, 
- Information hierarchy
- Recruitment Relevance
- Whether users could easily understand the purpose of the page.
- Whether users could understand Siena's role in the VISTA experience

## Key Actions

- Rewrote unclear content using more direct, plain-language wording.
- Updated outdated or inaccurate information.
- Improved organization of page content.
- Revised recruitment-related information to better support the VISTA user journey.
- Added and clarified information about the Siena VISTA program and member experience.
- Corrected content accuracy issues identified during the audit.

 ## Connecting Content to the User Journey

A key content challenge was helping users understand that the
VISTA experience involved more than their nonprofit host site.

Because VISTAs interact with AmeriCorps, Siena University, and their host organization, content needed to make Siena's role clearer and explain how the different parts of the experience connected.

The goal was to help users understand:

**AmeriCorps + Siena VISTA Program + Nonprofit Host Site**

This supported the broader product goal of making the website a useful resource throughout the recruitment and service journey.

## Recruitment-Focused Content

A major product change was the creation of an [**Open Positions**](./metrics/website-walkthrough.md)

The page provided prospective VISTAs with a direct way to discover available Siena VISTA opportunities through the website.

This created a new recruitment touchpoint and gave prospective users a concrete reason to visit the Siena VISTA website rather than relying entirely on other points in the recruitment process.

**Discover → Explore → Understand → Apply**

## Example

One identified issue involved inaccurate Education Award and Living Allowance information. 
The content was corrected to improve accuracy and reduce the risk of users relying on incorrect program information.

## Results

**35 content issues were identified and completed.**

Content improvements supported the broader goal of making the website a reliable and useful product 
rather than a static marketing asset.
